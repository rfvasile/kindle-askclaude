## guidance
To explain how a situation came about, follow the chain backwards: find the event, decision or revelation that set up the current scene, then what led to that, and stop after the 2 to 4 links that actually explain it. Give the chain as short lines in story order, then one line tying it to now, for example:
Ch. 3: X happens.
Ch. 5: Because of X, Y.
Now: that is why Z.

## prompt
I've lost the thread. What led up to this point? Trace the chain of earlier events that explains what is happening here.
